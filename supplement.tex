\section{Omitted Proofs for the TBA Construction}
\label{sec:supp-proofs}

\subsection{Reset Completion}
\label{subsec:reset-completion-proof}
Proof of Lemma~\ref{main-lem:reset-completion}. The extension $\rho'$ is
clock-consistent by construction. Consider a preservation clock $x$. If the
cube taken at position $i$ contains $\Unch(x)$, then neither $\rho$ nor
$\rho'$ resets $x$ at $i$; otherwise $\rho'$ resets it. Hence $\rho'$ resets
$x$ wherever $\rho$ does, and the clock recurrence
\eqref{main-eq:clock-update} gives, by induction on positions,
$\CVal_{\rho'}(i,x)\le\CVal_\rho(i,x)$. The literals over $x$ are upper
bounds, so remain true. Dually, $\rho'$ resets a restart clock only where
the cube contains $\Reset(x)$, hence only where $\rho$ resets it. Therefore
$\CVal_{\rho'}(i,x)\ge\CVal_\rho(i,x)$, and the lower-bound literals
remain true. Action literals and the B\"uchi condition are unchanged, so the
same run is accepting after completion (Coq lemma
\texttt{complete\_complete}).

\subsection{Exactness of Relaxation and Reset Completion}
Proof of Proposition~\ref{main-prop:reset-completion}. For soundness, a run of the
completed TBA gives a run of the relaxed automaton on the propositional word
of its clock-consistent extension. The reset and preservation markers agree
with the completed reset set, so the run satisfies every retained literal.
By language preservation of relaxation and the backend contract, this word
satisfies $\T(f)$. The soundness direction of
Theorem~\ref{main-thm:encoding-correctness} gives $w,0\satM f$.

For completeness, Theorem~\ref{main-thm:encoding-correctness} supplies a
clock-consistent extension of $w$ satisfying $\T(f)$. The backend contract
accepts its propositional word, and relaxation preserves that run. The
reset-completion lemma then supplies an extension accepted by the completed
TBA. The mechanized theorem is
\texttt{MTL\_to\_TBA\_correct\_with}.

\section{Auxiliary Lemmas of the Correctness Proof}
\label{sec:supp-aux}
\label{sec:supp-lemmas}
This section records the semantic and arithmetic facts used in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{main-thm:encoding-correctness}.

\subsection{Basic LTL Semantic Lemmas}
For every LTL formula $A$ and position $i$,
\begin{align}
\rho,i\satL \Always A &\Longleftrightarrow \forall j\ge i,\;\rho,j\satL A,\nonumber\\
\rho,i\satL \Eventually A &\Longleftrightarrow \exists j\ge i,\;\rho,j\satL A,\nonumber\\
\rho,i\satL \Always\Eventually A &\Longleftrightarrow
  \forall n\ge i\;\exists j\ge n,\;\rho,j\satL A,\label{eq:sem-GF}\\
\rho,i\satL A\WeakUntil B &\Longleftrightarrow
  \Bigl(\exists j\ge i.\nonumber\\[-1mm]
  &\qquad\rho,j\satL B\\[-1mm]
  &\qquad\land\forall k\in[i,j).\\[-1mm]
  &\qquad\quad\rho,k\satL A\Bigr)\nonumber\\
  &\qquad\lor\forall j\ge i.\;\rho,j\satL A.\nonumber
\end{align}
These are Coq lemmas \texttt{LG\_semantics}, \texttt{LF\_semantics},
\texttt{LGF\_semantics}, and \texttt{LW\_semantics}. Time divergence
adds the following useful consequence: if $d\ge0$ and
$\Always\Eventually B$ holds from $i$, some future $B$ occurs at physical
distance at least $d$ from $i$ (Coq: \texttt{GF\_far}).

\subsection{Clock Arithmetic}
Clock consistency turns symbolic guards into elapsed-time inequalities.
\begin{lemma}[Accumulation under preservation]
\label{lem:clock-accumulation}
If $x$ is not reset at any position in $[i,j)$, then
\[
\CVal_\rho(j,x)=\CVal_\rho(i,x)+\elapsed_w(i,j).
\]
\end{lemma}
Since clock values are nonnegative, $\elapsed_w(i,j)\le\CVal_\rho(j,x)$.
Thus an upper guard $x\le d$ at $j$ entails
\begin{equation}
\elapsed_w(i,j)\le d.
\label{eq:upper-clock}
\end{equation}
An upper guard $x<d$ gives the strict version.

\begin{lemma}[Clock value after a reset]
\label{lem:clock-after-reset}
If $x$ is reset at $i$ and $j\ge i+1$, then
$\CVal_\rho(j,x)\le\elapsed_w(i,j)$. If it is not reset again before $j$,
equality holds.
\end{lemma}
A later reset only makes the clock younger. Hence a lower guard gives
\begin{equation}
x\ge d\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
d\le\CVal_\rho(j,x)\le\elapsed_w(i,j),
\label{eq:lower-clock}
\end{equation}
and $x>d$ entails a strict lower bound. The Coq development proves these
facts as \texttt{preserve\_accumulate},
\texttt{after\_reset\_value\_le\_elapsed}, and
\texttt{reset\_preserve\_value\_eq\_elapsed}.

\subsection{Strict Bounds}
\label{subsec:strict-bounds}
The eight hatted constructors are the primitive proof families. Ordinary
timed operators have already been reduced by
Eqs.~\eqref{main-eq:derive-ule}--\eqref{main-eq:derive-rge}. Strict comparisons remain
strict through each translation and proof case. In particular, preservation
gives $\elapsed_w(i,j)\le\CVal_\rho(j,x)$, so $x<d$ implies
$\elapsed_w(i,j)<d$. After a reset,
$\CVal_\rho(j,x)\le\elapsed_w(i,j)$, so $x>d$ implies
$\elapsed_w(i,j)>d$.

The only strict case requiring more than direct guard arithmetic is the
fairness branch of $\hUntil_{>d}$. Time divergence gives a future $q$ at
distance at least $d$ from position $i+1$; strict timestamp increase gives
$t_{i+1}>t_i$, hence the selected $q$ is at distance strictly greater than
$d$ from $i$. This is the argument formalized in Coq lemma
\texttt{sound\_UhatGt}.
